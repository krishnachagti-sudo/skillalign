1. Sign in to GitHub and open https://github.com/krishnachagti-sudo/skillalign/edit/main/TEAM.md (GitHub offers to fork the repo; say yes).
2. Add one row to the table below: your name, your part, and one thing you would change in SkillAlign after trying the live site (https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/skillalign/).
3. Click "Commit changes", then "Create pull request". Krishna merges it.
